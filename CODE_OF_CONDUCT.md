# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

MSF Fusion is committed to providing a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone participating in the project, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and professional.
- Assume good intent, but prioritize clarity and evidence in technical discussions.
- Give and receive feedback gracefully; focus on ideas, code, and documented behavior.
- Respect privacy, security boundaries, and responsible disclosure practices.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or hateful conduct.
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberate misinformation, including fabricated benchmarks or security claims.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and documentation) and when representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and may take appropriate corrective action, including editing/removing comments, rejecting contributions, or restricting participation.

To report an incident, contact the maintainers through a private channel appropriate to the situation. For security vulnerabilities, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
